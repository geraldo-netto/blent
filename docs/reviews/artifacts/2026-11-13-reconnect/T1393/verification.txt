T1393 remembered Android retry without another attachment event — 2026-11-13

Cause and change
ConnectionAdmissionRun ends after a worker failure; previously no Android owner
restarted it without ACCESSORY_ATTACHED or manual Connect. ConfiguredConnection
now owns one Activity-scoped remembered-session observer. It arms only after
fresh authentication and confirmed remembered trust. A durable same-attempt peer
projection survives coalesced authenticated/end events, including first approval
whose persistence completes after authentication.

Natural failure retries after 2 seconds, doubles to a 30-second cap, and resets
to 2 seconds after successful authentication even when success/end coalesce.
Initial unknown, public-pin-only and unremembered failures do not arm recovery.
Changed/forgotten peers remain subject to approval. Explicit disconnect/deny/revoke,
intervening attempts and Activity scope cancellation disarm prior retry intent.
Disconnect also cuts off a retired generation during pending backoff. Failed
settings or quarantined native ownership cannot invent a replacement admission.

Ownership and limits
The observer retains only immutable attempt tokens, one proven public peer,
small boolean outcomes and one bounded delay. Helpers project transient full
ConnectionViews before returning; route metadata/credentials are not retained
across retry waits. Configured start requests capture only token + wasActive
before coroutine dispatch. The retry port takes/returns tokens and rechecks
eligibility after settings I/O. Snapshot lifetime reduction is not physical
erasure proof. ResourceLane retains the original native owner through cleanup;
logical completion, delay expiry and cancellation do not authorize early reuse.
Application-owned established links survive Activity destruction; retry observation
is Activity-owned and stops. No unattended process-death recovery is claimed.
